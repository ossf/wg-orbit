# ORBIT Supported Projects

Several projects within the ORBIT Working Group are chartered directly by LF Projects and have pre-standing agreements in place for their governance. No guidance from ORBIT may supersede the existing charters for these projects.

The following projects fall into this category:

- [Gemara](https://github.com/ossf/gemara)
- [Minder](https://github.com/mindersec)
- [Privateer](https://github.com/privateerproj/privateer)

The lead maintainer from each of these projects fills a seat on the ORBIT TSC per the [ORBIT WG Charter](../../CHARTER.md).

As part of continued membership within the ORBIT Working Group, we ask that these projects do the following:

- Solicit feedback from the ORBIT TSC any time a modification is proposed to the project's charter / governance
- Participate in ORBIT steering decisions through the project's TSC representative
- Provide routine updates at the ORBIT WG meeting

Projects must continue to meet the acceptance criteria for ORBIT, such as the interoperability mandate.
