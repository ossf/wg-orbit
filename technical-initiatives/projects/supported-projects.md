# ORBIT Supported Projects

Several projects within the ORBIT Working Group are charted directly by LF Projects and have pre-standing agreements in place for their governance. No guidance from ORBIT may superceded the existing charters for these projects.

As part of continued membership within the ORBIT Working Group, we ask that these projects notify the ORBIT TSC any time a modification is proposed to the project's charter.

Projects must continue to meet the acceptance criteria for ORBIT, such as the interoperability mandate.
